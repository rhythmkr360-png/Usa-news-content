# Privacy Policy — Military Brief

**Updated: October 17, 2026**

Military Brief is an independent Android news reader, not affiliated with the U.S. government, its military, or any news publisher.

**Device storage:** The app saves selected stories and the recently downloaded news feed locally on your device through Android app storage. It does not require an account or request device location.

**News delivery:** The app downloads a publicly accessible JSON feed hosted on GitHub. GitHub may handle network metadata such as IP address and request time under its own privacy notice. Opening an original article sends you to a third-party publisher, subject to that publisher's policies.

**Advertising:** Where enabled, Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent platform may process device details, advertising identifiers, IP address, consent signals, and interaction data as permitted by law and user settings. Consent is requested where legally necessary before requesting ads. You can review advertising privacy options from the About screen. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

**Your choices:** Remove saved stories in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, adjust the advertising consent choices where offered, or uninstall the app.

**Sharing:** The app operator does not run a user accounts database or sell user-provided data. Google's ad service and news publishers may process information under their separate policies.

**Support:** Public technical issues can be reported at https://github.com/rhythmkr360-png/Usa-news-content/issues . The developer must supply a current contact email or website before Play Store publication.

This document will be reviewed against the final Android build, ad settings, and Google Play Data safety declaration before release.
